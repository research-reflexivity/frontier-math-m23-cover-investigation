Arithmetic and geometry of an M23 Hurwitz scheme

François Le LayLast revision: 09/07/2026

An explicit characteristic-23 construction produces a lift with osculating incidence. A separate exact check on all seven covers, together with Galois invariance, characterizes the known fixed cover. Follow the local construction, its necessary checks, and the remaining open questions. Arrows show inputs: several may be needed together.
