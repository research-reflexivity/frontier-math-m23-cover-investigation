The Galois-fixed point on an M23 Hurwitz scheme: exact reconstruction and characteristic-23 geometry

François Le LayLast revision: 08/29/2026

This app maps the paper’s two established proofs of ε(Θ) = esing: an exact component calculation using certified branch cycles, and a branch-cycle-free comparison through an effective logarithmic quadratic line. It also isolates the proved limitations of ordinary nearby cycles and the ordinary coefficient Bockstein. Solid and dashed arrows are established; dotted arrows mark a non-implication or the separate open K-theoretic generalization.
